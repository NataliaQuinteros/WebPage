About

I'm Natalia

I'm a computer engineer from Universidad de Chile, graduated with highest honors, and I've spent the last few years building full-stack web applications end to end: APIs in Express and FastAPI, interfaces in React and Vue, and the integrations that hold the two together. Most recently at Crazycake Technologies, where I worked with clients directly from project planning through delivery.

The other half of my work is security. I research at CLCERT, the University of Chile's applied cryptography and cybersecurity laboratory, and wrote my thesis on detecting hidden spy devices through packet sniffing. I've taught seven semesters as a teaching assistant, including a competitive hacking workshop, and built course material for SheHacks, a Niñas Pro initiative that gets secondary-school girls into programming and cybersecurity.
